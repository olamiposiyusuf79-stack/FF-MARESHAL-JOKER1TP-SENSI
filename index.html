Device & Gameplay Tips
  • Display: Keep display refresh rate locked at 120Hz for consistent touch response.
  • DPI Scaling: Recommended DPI target: ~460 DPI for responsive drag drag distance.
  • Network: Use Cloudflare Private DNS (1dot1dot1dot1.cloudflare-dns.com) to stabilize ping.
  • Gameplay: Practice smooth drag speed rather than aggressive flicks to maximize headshot lock.
